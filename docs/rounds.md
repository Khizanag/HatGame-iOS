# The Three Rounds

A full game is **three rounds**. The defining twist of Hat Game: every round uses the **exact same words**, but the rule for how the explainer may convey them gets stricter each time. Players hear the whole word list three times, so by the later rounds the words are familiar and the challenge shifts to the method of explaining.

## The rounds and their rules

| Round | Rule | What the explainer may do |
|---|---|---|
| **Round 1** | No restrictions | Use any words, phrases, or descriptions — guess as many as you can. |
| **Round 2** | One word only | Say a single word as the clue. |
| **Round 3** | Gestures only | No speaking at all — gestures and miming only. |

The words do not change between rounds. Only the rule changes.

## How a round plays out

1. At the start of a round, the **hat is refilled** with the full set of words from setup.
2. The starting team takes a [turn](turn.md). When it ends, the next team takes a turn, and so on — teams rotate in order.
3. Turns keep alternating until the **hat is empty**. Emptying the hat ends the round.
4. The game advances to the next round (or, after Round 3, to the [final results](scoring.md#final-results)).

Because every word must be guessed before a round ends, every team gets multiple turns per round — as many as it takes to clear the hat between them.

## Who starts each round

- **Round 1** starts with the team chosen during [randomization](setup.md#6-randomize-and-start).
- **Each later round** starts with the team that **emptied the hat** to end the previous round. That team carries over (see below).

## Carrying over between rounds

The team that ends a round by guessing the last word is rewarded with continuity into the next round:

- **Leftover time is preserved.** Whatever time remained on that team's clock when the hat emptied is carried into its **first turn of the next round**. It is not given a fresh full timer for that turn.
- **The same explainer continues.** That team's explainer does **not** rotate at the round boundary — the same player keeps explaining.
- **Every other team resets.** All other teams begin the new round with a **full** timer, and their explainer rotation is unaffected by the round change.

A team that ends its turn on the timer (or by giving up) carries nothing forward — it gets a full timer the next time it plays.

> Why this matters: clearing the hat with time to spare is an advantage. You start the next round already in control, with a head start on the clock.

## Round transitions at a glance

```text
Round 1  (hat = all words, rule: no restrictions)
   teams alternate timed turns ... until hat is empty
   -> team that cleared the hat keeps leftover time + same explainer

Round 2  (hat refilled, rule: one word only)
   that team starts ... teams alternate ... until hat is empty
   -> team that cleared the hat keeps leftover time + same explainer

Round 3  (hat refilled, rule: gestures only)
   that team starts ... teams alternate ... until hat is empty
   -> Final Results
```
