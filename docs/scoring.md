# Scoring and Winning

Scoring in Hat Game is simple: every word a team guesses is worth one point. This page covers how points accumulate, how standings are shown, and how the game ends.

## Points

- A team scores **one point for each word its teammates correctly guess** (the "Got It" action during a [turn](turn.md)).
- Skipped words score nothing. Words left in the hat when time runs out score nothing.
- Points are tracked **per team, per round**. Because the same words are replayed each round, a team can score on the same word in all three rounds.

## Standings

Two views of the score are available at any time:

- **Round standings** — each team's points for a single round, ranked highest to lowest.
- **Total standings** — each team's points summed across all rounds so far, ranked highest to lowest.

During play, the **Check Standings** button on the [turn results](turn.md#turn-results) screen opens both without interrupting the flow. Between rounds you can see how a round finished and how the running total looks.

## Final results

After the third round ends:

- The game shows **Game Over** with the full **total scores**.
- The team with the **highest total** is the **Winner**.
- If two or more teams tie for the highest total, they are all shown as co-winners ("Winners!").

## Play again

From the final results you can:

- **Play Again** — keep the **same teams** and start over with **new words**. Players re-fill the hat (the previous words are cleared), then a fresh three-round game begins.
- **Return to Main** — leave the game and go back to the home screen.

## Worked example

Three teams, after all three rounds:

| Team | Round 1 | Round 2 | Round 3 | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Red | 8 | 6 | 5 | **19** |
| Blue | 7 | 7 | 6 | **20** |
| Green | 6 | 5 | 4 | 15 |

Blue wins with 20 total points, even though Red led after Round 1 — totals are cumulative across all three rounds.
