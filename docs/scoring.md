# Scoring and Winning

Scoring in Hat Game is simple: every word a team guesses is worth one point, and every word it skips costs the game's skip penalty. This page covers how points accumulate, how standings are shown, and how the game ends.

## Points

- A team scores **one point for each word its teammates correctly guess** (the "Got It" action during a [turn](turn.md)).
- A team **loses the skip penalty for each word it skips** — **1 point by default**, set per game to none, 1, 2, or 3 in [timer settings](setup.md#2-timer-settings). With skipping turned off there is no penalty.
- Penalties count in the round the skip happened, so a turn — and a team's total — can go below zero.
- Words left in the hat when time runs out score nothing and cost nothing.
- The [turn results](turn.md#turn-results) show the turn's net points, with the words guessed and skipped beneath when skips cost points.
- Points are tracked **per team, per round**. Because the same words are replayed each round, a team can score on the same word in all three rounds.

## Standings

Two views of the score are available at any time:

- **Round standings** — each team's points for a single round, ranked highest to lowest.
- **Total standings** — each team's points summed across all rounds so far, ranked highest to lowest.

During play, the **Check Standings** button on the [turn results](turn.md#turn-results) screen opens both without interrupting the flow. Between rounds you can see how a round finished and how the running total looks.

## Final results

After the third round ends:

- The game shows **Game Over** with the full **total scores**.
- The team with the **highest total** is the **Winner**.
- If two or more teams tie for the highest total, they are all shown as co-winners ("Winners!").

## Play again

From the final results you can:

- **Play Again** — a rematch: the **same teams** and the **same words**. Every score resets to zero, you choose (or randomize) the starting team again, and a fresh three-round game begins.
- **New Words** — keep the **same teams** but empty the hat. The game returns to [Fill the Hat](setup.md#3-fill-the-hat), players add new words, then a fresh three-round game begins.
- **Return to Main** — leave the game and go back to the home screen.

Neither option carries anything over from the finished game: scores, explainer roles, and leftover time all start fresh.

## Worked example

Three teams, after all three rounds:

| Team | Round 1 | Round 2 | Round 3 | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Red | 8 | 6 | 5 | **19** |
| Blue | 7 | 7 | 6 | **20** |
| Green | 6 | 5 | 4 | 15 |

Blue wins with 20 total points, even though Red led after Round 1 — totals are cumulative across all three rounds.
